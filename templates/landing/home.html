SCOPE

{% trans "What this platform solves for the department" %}

Four service areas handled end to end — from citizen submission to officer decision and department reporting.

{% trans "Land Acquisition & Compensation" %}

Land-related applications with a transparent, configurable compensation estimator. Every figure is an illustrative estimate subject to official verification.

{% trans "Disaster Relief" %}

Flood and other disaster relief applications with document capture, verification and status tracking from submission to decision.

{% trans "Welfare Schemes" %}

Government scheme applications with a rule-based eligibility check that explains every result for officer review — never an automatic rejection.

{% trans "Revenue Services" %}

Citizen applications, document verification and complete status tracking with notifications, remarks and downloadable acknowledgements.

WORKFLOW

{% trans "How it works" %}

A transparent six-step journey where AI assists and authorised officers decide.

1
Citizen submits an applicationSelects a service, fills the category form and uploads supporting documents.
2
AI processes the documentsOCR extracts name, address, document number and dates; missing or unreadable fields and possible duplicates are flagged.
3
Eligibility & compensationThe rules engine checks eligibility and the calculator estimates compensation where a land case applies, with a full breakdown.
4
Officer reviews the caseVerifies documents, corrects extracted data, requests missing documents and records the official approve or reject decision with a reason.
5
Citizen is notifiedSees the updated status, officer remarks and final decision in their dashboard, and can respond to correction requests.
6
Administrator monitorsViews live application statistics, department and scheme performance, audit trails and exportable CSV reports.
